    How Star Platinum works

    The SPHEREx telescope photographs the whole sky in infrared every six months. We line up its photos of one spot so you can see what changed. Things that stay put are stars and galaxies. Things that shift are the interesting ones.

    1. Pick a place. Type coordinates, spin the sky sphere, or press Random patch.
    2. Press play. Watch for a dot that jumps.
    3. Try Diff. White is new, black is gone.
    4. Hover a point for a quick look. Click to check if it is a known object.
    5. Open Analytics for counts, candidates and spectra.
